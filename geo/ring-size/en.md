# Ring Size Guide and International Size Converter

**Ring sizes are measured differently in every country, but they all describe the same thing: the inside of the ring. This guide from KAMBO Jewelry shows how to measure your size at home and how to convert it between US, UK, EU, Turkish and Japanese sizes, with the inner diameter and circumference in millimeters.**

## How to measure your ring size at home

**Method 1 — measure a ring that already fits (most accurate).**
Put a ring that fits the right finger on a ruler and measure the inside diameter, from inner edge to inner edge, in millimeters. Find that diameter in the chart below.

**Method 2 — measure your finger with a strip of paper.**
1. Cut a thin strip of paper, about 5 mm wide.
2. Wrap it around the base of your finger, snug but not tight. It should still slide over the knuckle.
3. Mark where the paper overlaps, then lay it flat and measure the length in millimeters. That is your circumference.
4. Find the circumference in the chart below.

Measure at the end of the day, when your fingers are warm. Fingers are smaller in the cold and in the morning. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one.

## Quick rules for converting between systems

- **EU size = inner circumference in mm.** EU 56 means 56 mm around the inside.
- **Turkish and Japanese size = circumference in mm − 40.** EU 56 is Turkish 16 and Japanese 16.
- **UK letters fall between US half sizes.** A UK letter without "½" sits a quarter size below the matching US size: UK P is about US 7¾, UK R is about US 8¾.
- **From diameter to circumference:** multiply by 3.14. A 22 mm inner diameter is about 69 mm around, which is US 12¾, EU 69, Turkish 29.

## Tips for the right fit

- **Wide bands fit tighter.** For a band 8 mm or wider, which is common in men's signet and gemstone rings, go up half a US size.
- **Large knuckles:** measure both the knuckle and the base of the finger and choose a size between the two, so the ring slides on but does not spin.
- **Thumb and index rings** are usually 2–4 US sizes larger than ring-finger sizes.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I convert a UK letter ring size to a US size?
UK letters sit between US half sizes. A letter with "½" matches a US half size (UK N½ = US 7, UK P½ = US 8), and a plain letter is about a quarter size smaller (UK P ≈ US 7¾, UK R ≈ US 8¾).

### What ring size is 22 mm inner diameter?
A 22 mm inner diameter is about 69 mm in circumference. That is roughly US 12¾, EU 69, Turkish 29 and Japanese 29.

### How are Turkish ring sizes calculated?
The Turkish ring size is the inner circumference in millimeters minus 40. For example, a ring with a 56 mm circumference is Turkish size 16, which is about US 7½.

### What is the difference between EU and US ring sizes?
The EU size is simply the inner circumference in millimeters, while the US size follows its own number scale. EU 54 is about US 7, EU 57 is about US 8, and EU 62 is about US 10.

### Should I size up for a wide ring?
Yes. For bands 8 mm or wider, choose about half a US size larger, because a wide ring covers more of the finger and feels tighter.
