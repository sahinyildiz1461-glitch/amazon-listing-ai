## Frequently asked questions

### Can you make a ring from my own sketch or photo?
Yes. Send your sketch, a photo of a ring you like, or just a description. We prepare a drawing or 3D render and a fixed price, and start production only after you approve the design.

### Do you make custom jewelry in gold and with diamonds, or only silver?
Both. Most of our pieces are 925 sterling silver, and we also make custom pieces in 14K and 18K gold, with natural diamonds and gemstones.

### Are your gemstones natural?
We use natural gemstones for most pieces. When a stone is treated or lab-created, we say so in the listing and in your quote, so you always know what you are buying.

### Is your silver real 925 sterling silver?
Yes. Our silver jewelry is solid 925 sterling silver and stamped 925. It is not silver-plated brass.

### How long does a custom piece take?
Production usually takes {{PRODUCTION_DAYS}} business days after you approve the design, plus shipping time to your country.

### Do you ship worldwide?
Yes. We ship from Türkiye to customers worldwide with tracked, insured delivery.

### Can you engrave names, dates or Arabic calligraphy?
Yes. We engrave names, dates, initials, and Arabic and Ottoman calligraphy, including verses. We engrave exactly the text you send and show it to you for approval first.

### How do I find my ring size?
Tell us your size in any system (US, UK letter, EU or Turkish) or the inner diameter of a ring that fits you, in millimeters. We convert it for you. For example, UK size P is about US 7¾ and an inner diameter of 22 mm is about US 12¾.

### Can you resize a ring or copy an old family piece?
Yes. We resize, replace stones, and remake heirloom or lost pieces from photos.
